The 8 Cardinal Vastu Directions & Elements

Understanding the cosmic energy balance of the Pancha Mahabhuta

North (उत्तर) Water / Kubera

Lord of Wealth & Opportunities. Ideal for living rooms, water bodies, and cash safes. Keep clutter-free.

North-East (ईशान्य) Water / Divine

The Ishanya zone governs mental clarity and spirituality. Most auspicious for Pooja room and meditation.

East (पूर्व) Air / Surya

Governs social connectivity, health, and vitality. Wide windows and open ventilation ensure positive solar prana.

South-East (आग्नेय) Fire / Agni

The sacred Agneya fire zone. Ideal position for the kitchen hob and electrical switchboards. Avoid water storage.

South (दक्षिण) Fire/Earth / Fame

Associated with relaxation, reputation, and goodwill. Suitable for bedrooms and solid storage walls.

South-West (नैऋत्य) Earth / Stability

The master zone of leadership, relationships, and grounding. Ideal for Master Bedroom and head of family.

West (पश्चिम) Space / Profits

Governs business profits, gains, and academic retention. Auspicious for dining rooms, study rooms, and safes.

North-West (वायव्य) Air / Support

Governs helpful friends, bank support, and movement. Suitable for guest bedrooms, finished goods, and parking.
