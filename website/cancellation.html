📅 Last Updated: October 2025 ⏱️ Cancel Before: Order Dispatch
You can cancel your order before it is dispatched from our warehouse. Once shipped, cancellation is not possible — but you may return the order after delivery under our Return & Refund Policy.

1. Order Cancellation by Customer

You may cancel your order at any time before it is dispatched from our warehouse. Once the order has been shipped and a tracking number has been assigned, cancellation is no longer possible.

To request a cancellation, please contact us immediately with your Order ID:

  • 📧 Email: support@astrovastusetu.com
  • 💬 WhatsApp: +91 78218 56342

We process orders quickly. For the best chance of successful cancellation, please contact us within 2 hours of placing your order.

💡 If your order has already been shipped, you can still initiate a return once the package is delivered to you.

2. Refund for Cancelled Orders

If your order is successfully cancelled before dispatch, a full refund will be processed to your original payment method:

Cancellation Scenario Refund Amount Refund Timeline
Cancelled before dispatch 100% Full Refund 5 – 7 Business Days
Order already shipped Not Applicable (Return after delivery) —
Cancelled by Astro Vastu Setu 100% Full Refund 3 – 5 Business Days

3. Cancellation by Astro Vastu Setu

We reserve the right to cancel any order for reasons including but not limited to:

  • Product stock unavailability or discontinuation.
  • Errors in product pricing or product description.
  • Fraud detection or payment verification issues.
  • Technical errors during order processing.
  • Inability to deliver to the provided address.

In such cases, you will be notified via email or phone and a full refund will be processed immediately without any deductions.

4. After Delivery

Post-delivery cancellations are not possible. However, if you are unsatisfied with your order, you may initiate a return within 7 days of delivery under our Return & Refund Policy.

⚠️ Please do not refuse delivery of your order. If you wish to return a product, accept the delivery first and then contact us to initiate a return. Refused deliveries are not covered under our refund policy.

5. Contact Us

For cancellation requests or queries, please contact us promptly:

  • 📧 Email: support@astrovastusetu.com
  • 📞 Phone / WhatsApp: +91 78218 56342
  • 🕒 Support Hours: Monday – Saturday, 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM
