1. Order Cancellation by Customer

You can cancel your order at any time before it is dispatched from our warehouse. Once the order has been shipped, it cannot be cancelled. However, you may return the order under our Return & Refund Policy once it is delivered to you.

To request a cancellation, please email us at support@astrovastusetu.com or contact us via WhatsApp at +91 98765 43210 with your Order ID.

2. Refund for Cancelled Orders

If you successfully cancel your order before it is shipped, the full amount will be refunded to your original payment method within 5-7 business days.

3. Cancellation by Astro Vastu Setu

We reserve the right to cancel any order for reasons including but not limited to:

  • Stock unavailability.
  • Errors in product pricing or description.
  • Fraud detection or payment verification issues.

In such cases, you will be notified, and a full refund will be processed immediately.
