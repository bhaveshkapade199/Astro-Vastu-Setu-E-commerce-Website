Q. Are the bracelets made of original stones? +
Yes, all our gemstone bracelets, Rudraksha, and other spiritual products are made from 100% genuine and natural materials. We carefully source them to ensure authenticity.
Q. How to choose the right bracelet? +
You can choose a bracelet based on your personal wellness goals, chakra balancing needs, or traditional spiritual beliefs. For example, 7 Chakra is for overall balance, Citrine for prosperity, and Rose Quartz for love and harmony. Read the product descriptions for detailed traditional benefits.

Our standard bracelets are stretchable and generally fit a wrist size of 6.5 to 7.5 inches. The bead sizes usually range from 8mm to 10mm. Specific sizes are mentioned on the respective product pages.

We process orders within 24 hours. Standard delivery typically takes 3-7 working days depending on your location in India. Express delivery takes 1-2 working days.
