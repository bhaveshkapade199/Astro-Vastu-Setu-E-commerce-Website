Shipping Policy

Home › Shipping Policy

1. Order Processing

All orders are processed within 24-48 business hours after receiving your order confirmation email. You will receive another notification when your order has shipped.

2. Shipping Rates and Estimates

We offer shipping across India. Shipping charges for your order will be calculated and displayed at checkout.

  • Standard Shipping: ₹99 (Free for orders above ₹999) - Estimated delivery: 3 to 7 working days.
  • Express Shipping: ₹99 - Estimated delivery: 1 to 2 working days (available in select pin codes).

Delivery delays can occasionally occur due to public holidays, extreme weather conditions, or unforeseen circumstances with courier partners.

3. Shipment Confirmation & Order Tracking

You will receive a Shipment Confirmation email/SMS once your order has shipped containing your tracking number(s). The tracking number will be active within 24 hours. You can also track your order via the Track Order page.

4. Damages

Astro Vastu Setu is not liable for any products damaged or lost during shipping. If you received your order damaged, please contact the shipment carrier to file a claim. Please save all packaging materials and damaged goods before filing a claim, and consider taking an unboxing video.
