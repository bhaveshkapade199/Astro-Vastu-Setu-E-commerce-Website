Once your order has been shipped, you will receive a Shipment Confirmation via email and/or SMS containing your tracking number(s). The tracking number will become active within 24 hours of dispatch.

All products are carefully packed to ensure they reach you in perfect condition. Our gemstone bracelets and spiritual products are wrapped in protective packaging to preserve their energy and quality during transit.

📹 We strongly recommend recording an unboxing video when you receive your package. This helps us quickly resolve any claims related to damage or missing items during transit.

Astro Vastu Setu is not liable for any products damaged or lost during shipping once the order has been dispatched and handed over to the courier partner. If you received your order in a damaged condition, please:

  • Save all original packaging materials and damaged goods.
  • Share your unboxing video with us at support@astrovastusetu.com within 48 hours of delivery.
