🕉️ Our Story

Astro Vastu Setu was founded with a simple yet powerful vision: to bring authentic, high-quality spiritual and wellness products to every home in India, making the ancient wisdom of gemstone healing and Vastu accessible to all.

Based in Jalgaon, Maharashtra, we source our products directly from trusted suppliers who share our commitment to quality and authenticity. Every bracelet, every Rudraksha bead, every Lakshmi Potli — is carefully selected and quality-checked before reaching you.

The name "Astro Vastu Setu" means "Bridge to Astro Vastu" — we aim to be the bridge between ancient spiritual wisdom and modern living, helping you bring positive energy, balance, and prosperity into your life.
