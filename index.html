Frequently Asked Questions

Are the stones natural and original? +
Yes, all our gemstones are 100% natural and genuine. We source directly from authentic suppliers and provide quality certification for our products.
How long does delivery take? +
Standard delivery takes 3-7 working days depending on your location. Express delivery (1-2 days) is available for select cities at an additional charge.
What is your return policy? +
We offer a 7-day easy return policy. If you're not satisfied with your product, you can return it within 7 days of delivery in its original packaging. Contact us on WhatsApp or email for return assistance.
Can I track my order? +
Yes! Once your order is shipped, you'll receive a tracking number via SMS and email. You can also track your order on our Track Order page using your Order ID and mobile number.
Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)? +
Currently we accept online payments through Razorpay — UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, and Wallets. COD may be available for select orders; please contact us on WhatsApp for details.
How should I care for my bracelet? +
Keep your bracelet away from water, perfumes, and harsh chemicals. Store it in the provided pouch when not in use. Clean gently with a soft dry cloth. Avoid exposure to direct sunlight for extended periods.
